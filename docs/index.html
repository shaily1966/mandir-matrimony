Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what families ask us most

Is Mandir Matrimony free to use?
Yes — creating a profile, entering your Kundali, and browsing matches is completely free. Premium features like unlimited messaging, detailed compatibility reports, and the AI biodata generator are available with a membership plan.
How accurate is the Kundali matching?
We use the Swiss Ephemeris — the same astronomical calculation engine used by professional Jyotish software worldwide — with Lahiri ayanamsa as standard. Our Guna Milan computation matches what trained practitioners calculate. However, Vedic astrology is a belief-based tradition; we always recommend consulting with a trusted pandit for final guidance.
Is this platform only for Hindus born in India?
No. Mandir Matrimony is a pan-Hindu platform open to all communities — Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali, and all sampradayas. We welcome second-generation diaspora born in Canada, the UK, the US, and elsewhere who follow Hindu traditions.
Can parents create a profile on behalf of their child?
Yes. Our platform is designed to support family involvement. Parents can create and manage a profile on behalf of their son or daughter, with appropriate consent. Family accounts can browse, save favourites, and communicate — this is a core feature, not an afterthought.
How do you protect my privacy and birth information?
Your birth details are used only for Kundali computation and are never shared publicly or sold to third parties. We operate under Canada's PIPEDA privacy framework. You control who can see your profile, photo, and contact details — nothing is visible by default until you choose.
Is this available outside Canada?
Yes — while we are built and headquartered in Canada, our platform serves the entire North American Hindu diaspora including the US. Many matches naturally cross the Canada-US border, and we have dedicated filters for cross-border compatibility including visa status.
