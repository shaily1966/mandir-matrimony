Our Story

Built by the
community, for it

Mandir Matrimony was built out of a real gap: the biggest matrimony platforms treat Kundali matching as a checkbox, and none of them truly understand what Hindu families in North America negotiate over — visa status, state or province, dual-culture values, and Vedic tradition all in the same conversation.

We serve three communities equally: Hindus living in the US, Hindus living in Canada, and Hindu families in India whose children are building lives in North America. Every feature — from the Swiss Ephemeris engine to the immigration filters — exists because it genuinely matters to all three.

Temple community roots Pan-Hindu, all traditions respected. No community is secondary.
